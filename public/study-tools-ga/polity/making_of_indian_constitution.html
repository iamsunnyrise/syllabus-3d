Introduction to Constitution & Forms of Government

Topics at a Glance

Constitution Kya Hota Hai? (Definition)

"A Constitution lays out certain ideals that form the basis of the kind of country a nation aspires to be."

💡 Hinglish Mein Samjho: Constitution (Samvidhan) ek aisi supreme rules aur aadarsho (ideals) ki kitab hai jo yeh batati hai ki koi desh bhavishya mein kaisa banna chahta hai aur wahan ki sarkar kaise chalegi.

⚠️ Very Important Conceptual Point (Statement Question in SSC):

"While all democratic countries are likely to have a Constitution, it is NOT necessary that all countries that have a Constitution are democratic."

👉 Matlab: Har लोकतांत्रिक (democratic) desh ke paas ek Constitution zaroor hota hai, lekin zaroori nahi ki jis desh ke paas Constitution ho wo democratic hi ho! (Jaise China ya North Korea ke paas apna Constitution toh hai, par wo democratic nahi hain).

Fast Facts Box

Origin & Nature

  • Tradition of Constitution began in: USA (in 1787)
  • Independence of USA: 4th July 1776
  • Nature of India's Constitution: Partly Rigid & Partly Flexible (Kuch articles aasani se amend hote hain, kuch ke liye tough special majority chahiye).

Other Forms of Government (Alag-Alag Prakar ki Sarkarein)

1. Communist

Source or means of production controlled by government.

Hinglish: Desh ki saari factories, resources aur production ke sadhano par seedha sarkar ka control hota hai.

Examples: China, Laos, North Korea, Cuba, Vietnam.
2. Monarchic

A government by a single person → specifically a King or a Monarch.

Hinglish: Jahan ek single vyakti yaani Raja ya Rani (Monarch) ka shasan chalta hai (Vanshavad/Hereditary).

Rule by: King / Queen (Monarch).
3. Totalitarian

Refers to total control over lives of citizens by whoever is ruling.

Hinglish: Tanashahi! Jo bhi shasan kar raha hai uska aam janta ki personal aur public life par 100% control hota hai.

Example: North Korea.
4. Oligarchic

A government of the few.

Hinglish: Jab poore desh ki satta kuch chune hue ameer ya behad taqatwar logo ke chhote group ke haath mein ho.

Examples: Iran, Russia.

Evolution of Constituent Assembly Demand (1928 – 1935)

Topics at a Glance
1928

Nehru Report (Early Draft)

The early draft of the Indian Constitution (Nehru Report) was submitted by the All Parties Conference at Lucknow → chaired by Motilal Nehru.

💡 Hinglish Note: Dhyan rahe, 1928 wali Nehru Report Motilal Nehru (Jawaharlal Nehru ke pita) ki adhyakshata mein Lucknow ke All Parties Conference mein pesh hui thi. Yeh Indian Constitution banane ki sabse pehli koshish (early draft) thi.

1934

First Idea of Constituent Assembly

M. N. Roy first proposed the idea of a Constituent Assembly for India.

💡 Hinglish Note: Exam mein baar-baar pucha jata hai ki Bharat ke liye ek alag "Samvidhan Sabha" (Constituent Assembly) honi chahiye, yeh vichar (idea) sabse pehle kisne diya? Jawab: M. N. Roy (1934).

1935

Official Demand by Congress

The Indian National Congress (INC) officially demanded a Constituent Assembly to frame the Constitution of India.

💡 Hinglish Note: M.N. Roy ke idea ke agle hi saal (1935 mein), Congress party ne pehli baar aadhikarik (officially) taur par Angrezo se Constituent Assembly ki maang ki.

Page 3 • British Proposals

Offers from Britain & Dominion Status

Topics at a Glance
Concept Clarity

Dominion Status Kya Hota Hai?

"It is a status given to certain countries that allowed them to be self-governing while remaining part of the British Empire. The term 'Dominion' means 'that which is mastered or ruled'."

👉 Simple Hinglish: Angrez kehte the ki aap apni sarkar khud chala lo (Self-governing), lekin aapka desh abhi bhi British Samrajya (British Crown) ke adheen hi rahega! Yaani azaadi adhoori thi.

Famous Comment on Dominion Status
"Dominion status is dead as a door nail."

— Said by Jawaharlal Nehru

(Nehru ji ne saaf keh diya tha ki Dominion Status ki maang ab lohe ki mari hui keel ki tarah khatam ho chuki hai, humein ab sirf Purna Swaraj chahiye!)

Offer #1 ❌ Rejected

1. August Offer: 1940

  • Proposed by British Viceroy Lord Linlithgow on August 8, 1940, to gain Indian support during World War II.
  • The offer promised Dominion Status for India after the war.

💡 WW-II mein Bharatiyo ka saath paane ke liye Linlithgow ne yeh lalach diya tha.

Offer #2 ❌ Rejected

2. Cripps Proposal: 1942

  • Offered India Dominion Status after the war.
  • Along with the creation of a Constituent Assembly to draft a new constitution.

💡 Stafford Cripps ne bhi war ke baad Dominion Status ka hi offer diya, isliye ise bhi thukra diya gaya.

Offer #3 ❌ Rejected

3. Wavell Plan: 1945

  • Also known as Shimla Conference.
  • Proposed by India's Viceroy, Lord Wavell (1945), to resolve the political deadlock by creating a new, interim Executive Council with equal representation for Hindus and Muslims.

💡 Congress aur Muslim League ke beech deadlock suljhane ke liye Shimla mein conference bulayi gayi thi.

!

Why were all three plans (1940, 1942, 1945) rejected?
Kyunki Congress ki maang "Purna Swaraj" (Complete Independence) thi, jabki Britishers baar-baar sirf "Dominion Status" offer kar rahe the!

Cabinet Mission Plan (1946), 389 Seats & Elections

Topics at a Glance
✅ Accepted Plan Year: 1946

Cabinet Mission Plan: 1946

Only Cabinet Mission Plan was accepted by both the Congress and the Muslim League, hence the Constituent Assembly of India was formed according to it!

Three British Cabinet Members:

1. Lord Pethick-Lawrence Chairman
2. Stafford Cripps Member (President of Board of Trade)
3. A. V. Alexander Member (First Lord of Admiralty)

🧠 Trick to Remember: P-S-A (Pethick, Stafford, Alexander) ya "CAL" (Cripps, Alexander, Lawrence).

Page 4 • Famous Speech

"Tryst with Destiny" (14th Aug 1947)

Delivered on the midnight of 14th August 1947 before the Constituent Assembly by Jawaharlal Nehru.

Nehru referred to Gandhiji as:

"The ambition of the greatest man of our generation has been to wipe every tear from every eye."

💡 Hinglish: Nehru ji ne Constituent Assembly mein apne mashhoor bhashan "Tryst with Destiny" mein Mahatma Gandhi ke baare mein kaha tha ki hamari peedhi ke sabse mahan vyakti ka sapna har aankh se har aansu ponchna raha hai.

Composition of Constituent Assembly (Total Seats Breakdown)

Cabinet Mission Plan ke tahat total 389 Members tay kiye gaye the:

Total Strength 389 Members
1. British India (Elected Members) 296 Seats
292 Governor's Provinces

Elected from 11 Governor Provinces of British India.

4 Chief Commissioner's Provinces

1 each from:
• Ajmer-Marwar
• Baluchistan
• Coorg
• Delhi

🧠 Trick for 4 Chief Commissioner Provinces: A - B - C - D (Ajmer-Marwar, Baluchistan, Coorg, Delhi).

2. Princely States (Deshi Riyasatein) 93 Seats
  • Nominated Members representing Princely States.
  • Not Elected — Rulers (Raja/Nawab) nominated them directly.
Exam Question: Constituent Assembly kaisi body thi?
👉 "It was partly elected and partly nominated." (296 Elected + 93 Nominated).

Rules of Seats Allocation (Page 5)

  • 3 Principal Communities: Seats allocated to each British province were divided among three principal communities: Muslim, Sikh, and General (All others except Muslim & Sikh).
  • Population Ratio: 1 seat was allotted per 1 million (10 Lakh) population.
  • Nature of Assembly: Partly elected and partly nominated.
  • Method of Election (Indirect Elections): Proportional Representation + Single Transferable Vote (Provincial Legislative Assembly ke members ne vote dala tha, aam janta ne direct vote nahi dala tha).

The Interim Government (Antarim Sarkar) of India was formed on 2nd September 1946. It lasted until India's independence on 15th August 1947, and the partition of the country into India and Pakistan.

Drafting Committee (29 Aug 1947) & The 3 Readings

Topics at a Glance
Set up on: 29th August 1947 Total Members: 7 (1 Chairman + 6 Others)
Chairman of Drafting Committee

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Also known as "Modern Manu" or "Modern Man"

Other 6 Members of Drafting Committee:

1. Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar
2. N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar
3. Muhammad Saadullah
6. K. M. Munshi
4. B. L. Mitter → Later replaced by N. Madhava Rau (due to ill health).
5. D. P. Khaitan → Later replaced by T. T. Krishnamachari (after Khaitan's death in 1948).
⭐ Super Important SSC Question

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Kahan Se Chun Kar Aaye The?

  • • Before Partition (July 1946): Elected to the Constituent Assembly from Bengal (Jessore-Khulna).
  • • After Partition (1947): Jab wo hissa East Pakistan mein chala gaya, he was elected from Bombay Presidency (Poona seat vacated by M.R. Jayakar).

Draft of Constitutional Assembly

21 Feb 1948

First Draft was published. It took 8 months for the people of India to discuss and suggest changes.

4 November 1948

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar introduced the Final Draft before the Constituent Assembly / public.

Three Readings of the Constitution in Constituent Assembly

1st Reading 5 Days 4th Nov 1948 – 9th Nov 1948

General discussion on the final draft.

2nd Reading (Longest) 10 Months & 3 Days 15th Nov 1948 – 17th Oct 1949

Clause-by-clause discussion & thousands of amendments.

3rd Reading Completed: 26 Nov 1949 Started 14th Nov – 26th Nov 1949

Motion to pass the Constitution was adopted on 26 Nov 1949.

1st Day Attendance 211 Members

Were present on the 1st day of the Constituent Assembly (9 Dec 1946).

Important Sessions Dates to Remember

⚡ Some Provisions Imposed Immediately on 26th November 1949:

(Articles 5 – 9)

ADOPTED ON Samvidhan Divas

26th November 1949

Constitution was adopted / enacted on this day. Celebrated every year as "Constitution Day" (Samvidhan Divas).

ENFORCED / COMMENCED ON Republic Day

26th January 1950

Constitution was fully enforced / commenced on this day. Celebrated as THE REPUBLIC DAY OF INDIA.

Why was 26th January chosen?
Kyunki 20 saal pehle 26th January 1930 ko pehla "Purna Swaraj Day" manaya gaya tha, following the historic Lahore Session of INC (December 1929)!

Working & Functions of the Constituent Assembly

Topics at a Glance

Working of Constituent Assembly (First Meeting)

1st Meeting Date: 9th December 1946 (211 Members)
Temporary President: (Oldest member) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha
Permanent President: (Elected 11 Dec 1946) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
Vice President: H. C. Mukherjee
First Parliament of India

Dual Functions of Constituent Assembly

Azaadi ke baad Constituent Assembly ne 'First Parliament of India' (Dominion Legislature) ke roop mein 2 kaam kiye:

1. As a Law-Making Body (To Legislate): Chaired by G. V. Mavalankar (who also became the 1st Speaker of Lok Sabha).
2. As a Constitution-Making Body: Chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad.

🧠 Yaad Rakhein: Jab Constitution banane ke liye baithti thi toh adhyaksh Rajendra Prasad hote the, aur jab aam kanoon (Law) banane ke liye baithti thi toh adhyaksh G.V. Mavalankar hote the!

Commonwealth Membership May 1949 Ratified India's membership of Commonwealth.

• Song: "Vande Mataram" (Bankim Chandra Chatterjee)
1st President of India Dr. Rajendra Prasad Elected as 1st President of India (took office on 26 Jan 1950).
