Official Text of the Preamble (English & हिंदी प्रस्तावना)

Topics at a Glance

💡 Exam Tip: SSC aur PCS exams mein Preamble ke exact words ka sequence (Sovereign → Socialist → Secular → Democratic → Republic) aur Hindi/English ke exact terms puche jaate hain. Dono ko dhyan se padhein:

The Constitution of India

PREAMBLE

"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its citizens:

JUSTICE, social, economic and political;

LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship;

EQUALITY of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all

FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation;

IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this twenty-sixth day of November, 1949, do HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CONSTITUTION."

📌 Original 1949 Image Text: "SOVEREIGN DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC" ✨ 1976 Added: Socialist, Secular, Integrity
भारत का संविधान

उद्देशिका (प्रस्तावना)

"हम, भारत के लोग, भारत को एक सम्पूर्ण प्रभुत्व-संपन्न समाजवादी पंथनिरपेक्ष लोकतंत्रात्मक गणराज्य बनाने के लिए, तथा उसके समस्त नागरिकों को:

सामाजिक, आर्थिक, और राजनैतिक न्याय;

विचार, अभिव्यक्ति, विश्वास, धर्म, और उपासना की स्वतंत्रता;

प्रतिष्ठा और अवसर की समता प्राप्त कराने के लिए,

तथा उन सब में व्यक्ति की गरिमा और राष्ट्र की एकता और अखंडता सुनिश्चित करने वाली बंधुता बढ़ाने के लिए

दृढ़संकल्प होकर अपनी इस संविधान सभा में आज तारीख २६ नवंबर, १९४९ ई० (मिति मार्गशीर्ष शुक्ल सप्तमी, सम्वत दो हजार छह विक्रमी) को एतद्द्वारा इस संविधान को अंगीकृत, अधिनियमित, और आत्मार्पित करते हैं।"

⭐ Hindi Calendar Date (Exam Fact): मिति मार्गशीर्ष शुक्ल सप्तमी, सम्वत २००६ विक्रमी (26 November 1949).

Nature & Compilation of the Preamble

Philosophy of Constitution: It tells us about the philosophy of the Constitution (Samvidhan ka darshan aur soch kya hai).

Summary / Essence: It contains the summary or the essence of the Constitution (Poore Samvidhan ka nichod/saar).

When was it enacted? It was made (enacted) AFTER the entire Constitution was compiled (Taaki yeh poore Samvidhan ke bilkul anuroop ho).

Based on Objective Resolution: It is a modified version of the Objective Resolution (उद्देश्य प्रस्ताव) presented by Jawaharlal Nehru before the Constituent Assembly (on 13 Dec 1946, accepted on 22 Jan 1947).

⭐ Most Repeated Exam Question ONLY 1 AMENDMENT

42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976

Preamble ko aaj tak sirf ek baar (Only Once) amend kiya gaya hai — 1976 mein 42nd CAA ke dwara, jab desh mein Emergency lagi hui thi aur Prime Minister Smt. Indira Gandhi thi.

3 New Words Added to Preamble in 1976:
1. Socialist
(समाजवादी)
2. Secular
(पंथनिरपेक्ष)
3. Integrity
(अखंडता)
🧠 Memory Trick: 1976 mein Indira Gandhi ji ne "SIS" (Sister) joda — Socialist, Integrity, Secular!

Page 3 • Understanding the Preamble

Source of Power & Nature of State: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular

Topics at a Glance
Source of Authority

"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA..." (हम, भारत के लोग...)

• This shows that the Constitution has been drawn up and enacted by the people through their representatives, and NOT handed down to them by a king or any outside powers.

Ultimate Authority in India People of India (भारत की जनता)
Nature #1 सम्पूर्ण प्रभुत्व-संपन्न

1. Sovereign (संप्रभु)

  • People have the supreme right to make decisions on internal as well as external matters.
  • No external power can dictate the Government of India.
💡 Simple Hinglish: Bharat kisi bhi videshi desh (jaise Britain ya USA) ka ghulam ya Dominion nahi hai. Apne desh ke andar aur bahar ke saare faisle lene ke liye hum 100% azaad hain!
Nature #2 (Added 1976) समाजवादी

2. Socialist (समाजवादी)

  • Wealth is generated socially and should be shared equally by society.
  • Government should regulate the ownership of land and industry to reduce socio-economic inequalities.
• Socialist Example: Russia
• USA is a: Capitalist Country (पूंजीवादी)
• India has: Mixed Economy (मिश्रित अर्थव्यवस्था)
💡 Hinglish: India ka समाजवाद (Democratic Socialism) Mixed Economy par chalta hai jahan सरकारी (Public) aur प्राइवेट (Private) dono sectors milkar kaam karte hain.
Nature #3 (Added 1976) पंथनिरपेक्ष

3. Secular (पंथनिरपेक्ष)

  • Citizens have complete freedom to follow any religion, or no religion at all.
  • There is NO official state religion of India.
  • Government treats all religious beliefs and practices (Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism, Jainism) equally and impartially.
⭐ Positive Concept of Secularism: In India, secularism follows a Positive Concept (Involvement of State in religious matters for social reform & equal protection of all religions).

Page 4 • Nature of State (Continued)

Democratic & Republic (India vs UK Comparison)

Topics at a Glance
Nature #4 लोकतंत्रात्मक

4. Democratic (लोकतांत्रिक)

  • The government functions according to a set of constitutional rules and principles.
  • People enjoy equal political rights, elect their rulers, and hold them accountable.
Abraham Lincoln's Famous Definition "A Government Of the People, By the People, For the People"

💡 Ultimate Authority: People of India (Satta ki asli chabi janta ke haath mein hai).

Nature #5 (Borrowed: France) गणराज्य

5. Republic (गणतंत्र / गणराज्य)

• A system of government in which the Head of the State is elected by the people (directly or indirectly) and NOT based on a hereditary succession (Vanshanugat/Raja-Rani nahi).

🇮🇳 INDIA: Democratic Republic Head of State (President) chunav ke zariye aata hai, koi bhi aam nagrik ban sakta hai!
🇬🇧 UK (Britain): NOT a Republic State! UK Democratic toh hai, par Republic nahi kyunki wahan Head of State Hereditary Monarch (King Charles) hai!

Page 4 • 4 Pillars of Objectives

Objectives of the Constitution: Justice, Liberty, Equality & Fraternity

Topics at a Glance
Objective #1 • 3 Types Taken from: Russia (USSR)

1. Justice (न्याय — 3 प्रकार)

• Citizens cannot be discriminated on the grounds of caste, religion, and gender. Social inequalities have to be reduced.

Three Types of Justice in Preamble:
1. Social
(सामाजिक)
2. Economic
(आर्थिक)
3. Political
(राजनैतिक)

⚠️ Exam Trap: "Religious Justice" Preamble mein NAHI hai! Sirf S-E-P (Social, Economic, Political) hai.

Objective #2 • 5 Forms Taken from: France

2. Liberty (स्वतंत्रता — 5 प्रकार)

• There are no unreasonable restrictions on a citizen in what they think, how they wish to express their thoughts, and the way they wish to follow up their thoughts in action.

Five Forms of Liberty in Preamble:
1. Thought (विचार) 2. Expression (अभिव्यक्ति) 3. Belief (विश्वास) 4. Faith (धर्म) 5. Worship (उपासना)

🧠 Order Trick: T - E - B - F - W (Pehle Thought aayega → phir Express karoge → Belief → Faith → Worship).

Objective #3 • 2 Types Taken from: France

3. Equality (समता — 2 प्रकार)

• All are equal before the law. The traditional social inequalities must end. The government should ensure equal opportunities for all.

Two Types of Equality in Preamble:
1. Equality of Status
(प्रतिष्ठा की समता)
2. Equality of Opportunity
(अवसर की समता)
Objective #4 • Brotherhood Taken from: France

4. Fraternity (बंधुता / भाईचारा)

• "All of us should behave as if we are members of the same family. No one should treat a fellow citizen as inferior."

Fraternity Assures Two Things:
1. Dignity of the Individual (व्यक्ति की गरिमा)
2. Unity and Integrity of the Nation (राष्ट्र की एकता और अखंडता — Integrity added in 1976)
🌍 Golden Borrowed Ideals Rule (Page 4)

• The terms "Liberty, Equality, Fraternity and Republic" are taken from FRANCE (French Revolution, 1789).
• The Ideals of Justice (Social, Economic & Political) are taken from RUSSIA (USSR) (Russian Revolution, 1917).

Page 5 • Landmark Judgments & Legal Status

Is Preamble a Part of Constitution & Is It Amendable?

Topics at a Glance
Case #1 • Year: 1960 ❌ NOT a Part

1. Berubari Union Case: 1960

(Indo-Pak transfer of Berubari Union territory near West Bengal / East Pakistan):

Supreme Court held that:

  • The Preamble is NOT a part of the Constitution & hence cannot be amended.
  • It is just a key to understanding the Constitution (mind of the constitution makers).
  • It is not legally enforceable.
💡 1960 mein Supreme Court ne kaha ki Preamble Samvidhan ka hissa nahi hai, isliye isme sanshodhan (amendment) nahi ho sakta.
Case #2 • Year: 1973 ✅ IS a Part & Amendable

2. Kesavananda Bharati Case: 1973

(Largest Constitutional Bench in Indian History):

  • A historic bench of 13 Judges overruled the Berubari judgment and gave the verdict that the Preamble IS a part of the Constitution and hence it CAN be amended.
  • It reflects the Basic Structure (मूल ढांचा) of the Constitution (Amendment cannot destroy the Basic Structure).
⭐ 1973 ke is faisle ke baad hi 1976 mein 42nd Amendment dwara Preamble ko amend kiya gaya!
Case #3 • Year: 1995 ✅ Reaffirmed

3. LIC of India Case: 1995

(Supreme Court Final Confirmation):

  • Reaffirmed the Kesavananda Bharati case ruling (Supreme Court ne dobara pukhta kiya ki Preamble Constitution ka ek abhinn ang / integral part hai).
🧠 Chronology Trick:
• 1960 (Berubari): No (Not a Part)
• 1973 (Kesavananda - 13 Judges): Yes (Part & Amendable)
• 1995 (LIC Case): Yes Reaffirmed!
⚖️ Enforceability of Preamble (Page 5)

Kya Preamble ko Court mein लागू (Enforce) karwaya ja sakta hai?

  • Not Enforceable in Court: It is NOT enforceable in a Court of Law (Yaani agar Preamble mein likhi kisi baat ka ullanghan ho, toh aap seedhe Preamble ke aadhar par Supreme Court/High Court mein writ jari nahi karwa sakte — it is Non-Justiciable).
  • Neither Power Nor Restriction: It is neither a source of Power nor a source of Restriction to the legislature (Sansad ko na toh isse koi extra power milti hai, na hi isse Sansad ki power rukti hai).
  • Aid to Legal Interpretation: However, it can be considered as an aid to the legal interpretation of the Constitution (Jab kisi Article ki bhasha unclear ho, toh Judges Preamble ki madad se uska sahi arth nikalte hain).
Preamble Quick Check
Part of Constitution? ✅ YES (1973)
Amendable? ✅ YES (Art 368)
Times Amended? 1 Time (1976)
Enforceable in Court? ❌ NO
Source of Power? ❌ NO
