Lengthiest Written Constitution & Parliamentary Government

Topics at a Glance
Salient Feature #1

1. Indian Constitution is the Lengthiest Written Constitution in the World

With 448 Articles and 12 Schedules, the Indian Constitution is the Longest Written Constitution of any Sovereign country in the world.

Original Constitution (1950)
  • • 395 Articles
  • • 22 Parts
  • • 8 Schedules
Present Constitution
  • • 448 Articles
  • • 25 Parts
  • • 12 Schedules

22 Parts se 25 Parts Kaise Hue? (Important Exam Math)

  • ❌ Deleted Part: Part 7 (Part VII) has been deleted (by 7th Amendment Act, 1956). (22 - 1 = 21)
  • ✅ 4 New Parts Added: Part IV-A (Fundamental Duties), Part IX-A (Municipalities), Part IX-B (Co-operative Societies), and Part XIV-A (Tribunals). (21 + 4 = 25 Parts!)
Salient Feature #2

2. Parliamentary Form of Government

• A Parliamentary Form of Government is a form of democracy in which the political party that wins the most seats in a national election forms the ruling government.

🌍 Borrowed From: UK (United Kingdom)

💡 Hinglish: Jahan Lok Sabha chunav mein sabse zyada seats jeetne wali party sarkar banati hai (PM + Council of Ministers), use Parliamentary System kehte hain. Yeh humne Britain (UK) se liya hai.

Key Term Definition

What is an "Amendment" (Sanshodhan)?

"A formal change to a law, contract, constitution, or other legal document."

👉 Samvidhan ya kanoon mein samay ke saath formal badlav ya sudhar karne ko Amendment kehte hain.

Written Constitution vs Unwritten Constitution

📖 Written Constitution (Likhit)

Ex: India, USA
  • ✅ Compiled and structured in a systematic form (Ek kitab mein vyavasthit dhang se likha hota hai).
  • ✅ Formally constituted (Ek Constituent Assembly dwara banaya gaya).
  • ✅ Not easy to Amend (Ise badalna aasan nahi hota, special process lagta hai).
  • ✅ Example: India

📜 Unwritten Constitution (Alikhit)

Ex: United Kingdom (UK)
  • • Not structured in a single book (Alag-alag traditions aur court decisions par aadharit).
  • • Not formally constituted at one single time.
  • • Easy to Amend (Aam kanoon ki tarah aasani se amend ho jata hai).
  • • Example: United Kingdom (UK)

3. Federation with a Strong Centre (Federal vs Unitary)

Topics at a Glance
Concept of Federalism

What is Federalism (Sanghvad)?

• Federalism: This refers to the existence of more than one level of Government in the country.

⭐ Golden Principle: "Under federalism, the states are NOT merely agents of the federal government; they derive their authority from the Constitution itself."

💡 Hinglish: Federalism ka matlab hai desh mein ek se zyada level par sarkar hona (Centre + State). Rajya sarkarein Kendra ki naukar ya agent nahi hain, unhe apni shakti seedhe Samvidhan se milti hai!

Pyramid of Governance

Three Tiers of Government Exist in India:

1. Union / Centre Government National Level
2. State Governments State Level
3. Local Bodies (Panchayats & Municipalities) Grassroots Level

Federal Features (Sanghiya Gun)

Power Sharing

Jo features States ki power aur autonomy ko protect karte hain:

  • 1 Written Constitution (Sab kuch likhit hai taaki Centre states ka haq na chheene)
  • 2 Supremacy of the Constitution (Samvidhan sabse upar hai)
  • 3 Rigidity of Constitution (Federal structure badalne ke liye States ki manzoori chahiye)
  • 4 Independence of Judiciary (Centre-State dispute suljhane ke liye azaad Supreme Court)
  • 5 Bicameralism (2 Sadan: Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha jo States ko represent karti hai)
  • 6 Dual Government (Centre Govt + State Govt)

Unitary Features (Ekatmak Gun)

Strong Centre

Jo features Centre (Union) ko zyada powerful banate hain:

  • 1 Single Constitution (Centre aur States dono ke liye ek hi Samvidhan)
  • 2 Single Citizenship (Sirf Bharatiya Nagrikta, state ki alag citizenship nahi)
  • 3 Integrated Judiciary (Top par Supreme Court, uske neeche High Courts)
  • 4 All India Services (IAS, IPS, IFoS ko Centre bharti karta hai aur States mein bhejta hai)
  • 5 Emergency Provisions (Emergency lagte hi saari power Centre ke haath mein aa jati hai)
  • 6 Appointment of Governors by Centre (State ke Governor ko President niyukt karte hain)

Topics at a Glance
Organ #1

Legislative (Vidhayika)

👉 Makes Laws

(Parliament & State Legislatures — Inka kaam desh ke liye naye kanoon banana hai).

Executive (Karyapalika)

👉 Carries Out Laws

Page 4 • Detailed Breakdown

Schedules 1 to 6 of Indian Constitution (With Ladakh News)

Topics at a Glance
Schedule 1 (T = Territory)

Names of States & Union Territories

• Names of States and UTs and their territorial jurisdiction (Koun sa rajya kahan tak phaila hai, uski seema aur naam).

Schedule 4 (R = Rajya Sabha)

Allocation of Seats in Rajya Sabha

• Allocation of Seats in Rajya Sabha to the States and the Union Territories (Har State/UT ko population ke hisab se kitni Rajya Sabha seats milengi).

Schedule 2 (E = Emoluments)

Emoluments, Allowances & Privileges (Salary/Bhatte)

Provisions relating to emoluments and allowances of 9 dignitaries:

1. President 2. Vice President 3. Governor 4. Speaker 5. Deputy Speaker 6. Chairman (RS/LC) 7. Deputy Chairman 8. Judges (SC & HC) 9. CAG
Schedule 3 (A = Affirmation & Oath)

Oaths and Affirmations (Shapath)

Forms of Oaths and Affirmations (Union + State) of:

1. Ministers (Union + State) 2. MPs (Sansad) 3. MLAs / MLCs 4. Judges (SC & HC) 5. CAG

⚠️ Exam Trap Note: President (Art 60), Vice-President (Art 69) aur Governor (Art 159) ki Oath Schedule 3 mein nahi, balki unke alag Articles mein di gayi hai!

Schedule 5 (S = Scheduled Areas)

Administration of Scheduled Areas & Scheduled Tribes

Provisions relating to the administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in 10 States:

1. Andhra Pradesh
2. Telangana
3. Chhattisgarh
4. Madhya Pradesh
5. Jharkhand
6. Odisha
7. Maharashtra
8. Gujarat
9. Rajasthan
10. Himachal Pradesh

🧠 Easy Grouping Trick:
• Pairs: (Andhra + Telangana), (MP + Chhattisgarh), (Maharashtra + Gujarat), (Jharkhand + Odisha) = 8 States + Himachal & Rajasthan = Total 10 States!

Schedule 6 (O = Other Tribal Areas)

Administration of Tribal Areas of 4 States

Provisions related to administration of tribal areas in 4 North-Eastern States:

Assam
Tripura
Meghalaya
Mizoram

🧠 Trick: "ATM Square (AMTM)" — Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram (⚠️ Manipur is NOT in 6th Schedule!)

🔥 Current Affairs Connection LADAKH DEMANDS

Why Schedule 6 was in News?

The Sixth Schedule has been in the news recently due to protests in Ladakh demanding its extension to the region (along with Statehood) to protect its tribal culture, land, and autonomy.

What the Sixth Schedule Entails (Kya milta hai isme?):
  • • Autonomous District Councils (ADCs) (Chune hue local tribal councils)
  • • Powers of ADCs (Zameen, jungle, aur local kanoon banane ki shakti)
  • • Cultural Preservation (Tribal sanskriti aur pehchan ki suraksha)

Page 5 • Division of Legislative Powers

Schedule 7: Union, State, Concurrent & Residuary Lists

Topics at a Glance

📌 Schedule 7 (Federal Provision): Contains Three Lists containing subjects on which the Union and States can make laws.

List #1 • Centre Makes Laws

1. Union List (Sangh Soochi)

Initially 97 Subjects
Currently 100 Subjects

Important Subjects in Union List:

Defence Banking Foreign Affairs Citizenship Atomic Energy Airways Census (Jangana) Arms & Ammunition Inter-State Trade & Commerce Labour & Mines Regulation
List #2 • States Make Laws

2. State List (Rajya Soochi)

Initially 66 Subjects
Currently (-5) 61 Subjects

Important Subjects in State List:

Public Order (Police) Local Government Public Health Pilgrimage (Teerth) Liquor (Sharab) Agriculture (Krishi) Fisheries Betting and Gambling
List #3 • Both Union & State

3. Concurrent List (Samvarti)

Initially 47 Subjects
Currently (+5) 52 Subjects

Important Subjects in Concurrent List:

Education Electricity Marriage and Divorce Insolvency & Bankruptcy Trade & Labour Dispute Weights and Measures
⭐ Most Asked SSC Question

5 Subjects Transferred from State List (66→61) to Concurrent List (47→52)

Transferred by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976:

1. Education (Shiksha)
2. Forests (Jungle)
3. Weights & Measures
4. Protection of Wild Animals & Birds
5. Administration of Justice

🧠 Hinglish Story Trick: Jungle (Forest) mein Jaanwaro/Pakshiyo (Wild Animals & Birds) ko Education di gayi ki sahi Weight & Measure se Justice kaise karein!

4th Category of Subjects

Residuary List / Powers (Avshisht Shaktiyan)

  • • What are they? Subjects that are NOT included in Union, State, or Concurrent List (e.g., Cyber Laws, Space, AI).
  • • Who has the power to make laws? Parliament has the exclusive power to legislate on residuary subjects (Article 248).
  • • Borrowed From: Canadian Constitution (Canada).
💡 Jo vishay 1950 mein socha nahi gaya tha aur teeno list mein nahi hai, us par kanoon banane ka haq sirf Parliament ke paas hai!

5 Scenarios in Which Parliament / Union Can Legislate on State List Subjects

Waise toh State List par State Govt kanoon banati hai, lekin in 5 exceptional cases mein Parliament bhi State List par kanoon bana sakti hai:

1 During National Emergency (Article 250)
2 During President's Rule in a State (Article 356)
3 If two or more States request/ask the Centre to make a law for them (Article 252)
4 To enforce International Treaties / Agreements (Article 253)
5 If Rajya Sabha gives Parliament this power with Special Majority in national interest (Article 249)

Schedule 8 (Official Languages) & Classical Languages of India

Topics at a Glance
Schedule 8 (O = Official Languages) 14 → 22 Languages

Evolution of 22 Scheduled Languages

Originally (1950) 14 Languages
Presently 22 Languages

4 Key Constitutional Amendments to Remember:

1. 21st CAA, 1967 +1 Language (15th)

Sindhi was added.

2. 71st CAA, 1992 +3 Languages (18)

Konkani, Manipuri, and Nepali were added.

🧠 Trick: "NAMAK" (Nepali, Manipuri, Konkani)
3. 92nd CAA, 2003 +4 Languages (22)

Bodo, Dogri, Santhali, and Maithili were added.

🧠 Trick: "BDSM" (Bodo, Dogri, Santhali, Maithili) & 21 + 71 = 92nd CAA!
4. 96th CAA, 2011 Name Change

It changed the name 'Oriya' to 'Odia'.

6 Earlier + 5 Added in 2024 = 11 Total

Classical Languages of India (Shastriya Bhashayein)

Earlier 6 Classical Languages:
  • 1. Tamil (2004 - Oldest)
  • 2. Sanskrit (2005)
  • 3. Telugu (2008)
  • 4. Kannada (2008)
  • 5. Malayalam (2013)
  • 6. Odia (2014)
🔥 Newly Added in 2024 (5):
  • 7. Marathi
  • 8. Pali
  • 9. Prakrit
  • 10. Assamese
  • 11. Bengali
Total Now = 11 Languages!
🧠 Trick to Learn First 6 Classical Languages:

"Tu Shuru To Kar Mai Aariya (Oriya)"

• Tu = Tamil | Shuru = Sanskrit | To = Telugu | Kar = Kannada | Mai = Malayalam | Aariya = Odia (Oriya)!

Schedules 9, 10, 11 & 12 (Added After 1950)

Topics at a Glance
Schedule 9 (L = Land Provision) 1st CA: 1951

Land Reforms & Abolition of Zamindari System

• Provisions related to Land Reforms and Zamindari System.
• Added through the 1st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1951 (during PM Jawaharlal Nehru's tenure).

💡 Hinglish: Original Constitution mein 8 hi Schedules the. Sabse pehla naya Schedule (9th Schedule) 1951 mein Zamindari pratha khatam karne ke liye joda gaya.

Schedule 10 (D = Defection) 52nd CA: 1985

Anti-Defection Law (Dal-Badal Virodhi Kanoon)

• Provisions relating to disqualification of MPs and MLAs on grounds of defection (party badalne par sadasyata radd hona).
• Added by the 52nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1985.

💡 Trick: 10th Schedule = Das = Dal-Badal (Defection)!

Schedule 11 (P = Panchayat) 73rd CA: 1992

Panchayati Raj Institutions (Rural Local Govt)

• Provisions relating to Panchayats.
• Added by the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.
• Contains 29 Functional Matters (Subjects).

🧠 Trick for 29 Matters: 11th Schedule mein 2 + 9 = 11 (29 Functional Matters)!
Schedule 12 (M = Municipality) 74th CA: 1992

Municipalities (Urban Local Govt / Nagar Palika)

• Provisions relating to Municipalities.
• Added by the 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.
• Contains 18 Functional Matters (Subjects).

⭐ Quick Compare: Panchayat (Sch 11) = 29 Matters | Municipality (Sch 12) = 18 Matters.
