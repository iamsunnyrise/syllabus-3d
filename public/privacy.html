Privacy Policy for Syllabus 3D

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Syllabus 3D ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our web application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our website at https://syllabus-3d-zeta.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Syllabus 3D, we may collect the following information:

  • Google Account Information: If you choose to log in using Google OAuth / Google Identity Services, we access your name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google to authenticate your identity.
  • Study Data: Your preparation data including subjects, topics, completion progress, revision logs, pomodoro sessions, and notes are stored securely on your browser device and synced with cloud services only when you are signed in.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your user account and verify your identity.
  • To display your personalized preparation dashboard, revision schedule, and syllabus analytics.
  • To back up your study progress across your authorized devices.

3. Google User Data Policy

Syllabus 3D complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your personal data or use your Google data for targeted advertisements.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB and LocalStorage) and encrypted during transmission using HTTPS/TLS protocols. You have complete control over your study data and can export or delete your account data anytime from the Settings panel.

5. Third-Party Services

We use standard cloud providers including Google Cloud Platform and Vercel to host and run our web application. These services process data in accordance with their respective security and privacy standards.
